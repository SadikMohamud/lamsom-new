LAMSOM, Lambeth Somali Community Association

Lambeth Somali Community AssociationBilingual advice, education and support for families in Stockwell.

Serving Stockwell since1990

A safe, welcoming home for the Somali community in Lambeth. Advice, learning, health and sport, all under one roof on Stockwell Road.

Somali proverb

Aqoon la'aan waa iftiin la'aan

Without knowledge, there is no light.

Ignorance is like living in darkness, while knowledge brings clarity, guidance and understanding. It is why education sits at the heart of everything we do.

Programmes and services

What we offer

Six projects run by and for the community, from advice sessions to Saturday football.

Advice

Advice and employment support

Tailored, bilingual advice on housing, benefits and work, plus help with job applications, CV writing and career planning for economic stability and personal growth.

Cancer screening and early detection
