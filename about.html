About us

Rooted in
Stockwell

The Lambeth Somali Community Association (LAMSOM) supports the Somali community in Lambeth with bilingual advice and advocacy, mainly for families, women and young people.

Who we help

Most of the people we support are women and young people, and we increasingly help older people and single parents, meeting their particular needs and promoting their wellbeing.

Our mission

To build unity, empowerment and growth within the Somali community in Lambeth through dedicated services, cultural programmes and educational opportunities.

Our school

Our supplementary school gives academic support in core subjects like maths, English and science.

1990

Our history

From small beginnings
to a thriving hub

Founded in 1990, LAMSOM has been a cornerstone for the Somali diaspora, creating a safe and welcoming space for all. Our journey is a testament to our community's resilience.
